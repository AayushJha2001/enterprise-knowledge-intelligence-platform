Information Security Policy

Policy SEC-201. Owner: Information Security. Applies to all employees, contractors and service accounts with access to Northwind systems.

Passwords and sign-in

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Use the company password manager to generate and store them; never reuse a work password on another site.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN, the HR portal and all production systems. Use the authenticator app; SMS codes are not accepted.

If you forget your password, reset it at the self-service portal. After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes.

Remote access

Connect through the corporate VPN whenever you work outside the office. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the VPN switched on.

If the VPN shows error VPN-809, your device certificate has expired: open the device management app and choose Renew certificate, then restart the VPN client.

Reporting incidents

Report suspected phishing, lost devices or data leaks to the security team within 1 hour at security@northwind.example or by calling extension 4444. Do not try to investigate yourself, and do not delete the suspicious email.
