# Northwind Employee Handbook

This handbook applies to all permanent employees of Northwind Traders. Contractors should
refer to their agency agreement. Policy owner: People Team (HR-100).

## Working hours

### Core hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours you
may arrange your day freely, as long as you work 40 hours per week on average over a month.

### Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements need
written approval from your manager and the People Team. Remote work from another country is
limited to 20 working days per calendar year because of tax and insurance rules.

## Leave

### Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public
holidays. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours. Up to
5 unused days can be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March.

### Sick leave

Tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A doctor's note is required
from the fourth consecutive day of sickness. Sick days do not reduce annual leave.

### Parental leave

The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary
caregiver receives 6 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken in up to three blocks
within the first year after the birth or adoption. Leave must be requested at least 8 weeks
in advance through the HR portal.

## Expenses

### Travel

Book flights and trains through the travel portal. Economy class is required for flights
under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights. Hotel stays are capped at
150 EUR per night, or 220 EUR in London, Paris, New York and Zurich.

### Claims

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the purchase, with an itemised receipt for anything
above 25 EUR. Claims are reimbursed with the next monthly salary payment. Alcohol is not
reimbursed except at client events approved in advance by a director.

## Equipment

New employees choose a 14-inch or 16-inch laptop and receive a 500 EUR home-office budget
for a monitor, chair or desk. Equipment remains company property and must be returned
within 10 working days of leaving the company.
